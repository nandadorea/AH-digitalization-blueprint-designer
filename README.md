# AH Digitalization Blueprint Designer

## A Design System for Digitalizing Animal Health Surveillance

The AH Digitalization Blueprint Designer is a methodology for documenting, designing and implementing the digital transformation of animal health surveillance capabilities.

Rather than prescribing software solutions, the Design System provides a structured language for describing surveillance capabilities, implementation building blocks, implementation assets, interoperability standards and supporting resources in a consistent, reusable and technology-independent manner.

The Design System serves three complementary audiences:

- Veterinary Services planning digital transformation.
- Technical teams implementing surveillance solutions.
- AI systems generating, validating and maintaining Digitalization Blueprints.

The methodology is intentionally designed to be both human-readable and machine-readable, enabling reproducible development of technical guidance and future AI-assisted authoring.

The Design System is being developed using an Example-Driven Design approach. Normative concepts are refined through worked example Blueprints before being incorporated into the Design System. This ensures that specifications are grounded in practical implementation rather than theoretical design.

Veterinary Services have access to a growing number of standards, technical guidelines, templates and digital tools. However, these resources are often developed independently and provide limited guidance on when, why and how they should be used together during digital transformation.

Digitalization Blueprints organize these resources into structured design journeys centred on business capabilities, helping countries make informed implementation decisions while adapting solutions to their own context.

---

## Purpose: decision-support framework for digital transformation

The Animal Health Digitalization Blueprint Designer is a design system for creating Digitalization Blueprints. Rather than prescribing software implementations or organizational workflows, it provides a decision-support framework that guides Veterinary Services through the design of digital capabilities adapted to their own context.

Blueprints are structured design guides that facilitate informed implementation decisions. They focus on business capabilities rather than specific technologies, workflows or organizational models, and are supported by reusable decision-support resources and implementation assets.

---

## Objectives

The Design System aims to:

- establish a common language for digitalization;
- promote interoperability and reuse;
- separate surveillance capabilities from software implementation;
- support countries throughout the implementation lifecycle;
- ensure consistency across all Digitalization Blueprints;
- provide a stable foundation for future technical guidance.
